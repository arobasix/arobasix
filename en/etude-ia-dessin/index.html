Between trace, colour and interpretation

The drawing cannot be reduced to a single image. The black and white version shows the architecture of the lines, while the colour fragment preserves a more physical sensation of matter and perception.

The AI intervention does not claim to recover a lost photographic truth. It proposes a study: clarifying, bringing elements closer, extrapolating at times and making relationships between forms easier to see.

The colour images are AI-assisted interpretations based on the available documents. The material original remains the primary reference.
