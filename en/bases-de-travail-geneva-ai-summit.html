Workshop Preparation Notes

Shared Prosperity and Trust in AI

EventGeneva AI Summit 2027
WorkshopWednesday, 10 February 2027 · 14:00–15:00 CET

Many obstacles can hinder shared prosperity and trust in AI. The following four points are offered as a practical basis for the discussion.

1. The myth of artificial intelligence as an exterminator

It may be more useful to examine the risk posed by malicious people or groups using connected AI systems than to focus exclusively on the unlikely scenario of an AI independently eliminating humanity.

2. Fear of losing power, social role or control

People may fear an intelligence they do not fully understand, especially when it is already present in almost every area of life. This point also requires a precise discussion of where AI is already superior to human intelligence.

3. Data-centre growth and possible solutions

Reducing the physical scale of data centres while increasing computing power is one possible response to the environmental and infrastructure problems created by very large facilities.

4. AI in authoritarian and totalitarian states

What happens in Russia, North Korea, Iran and other authoritarian contexts? Concrete examples are welcome, especially concerning surveillance, facial recognition and the uses of AI by state institutions.
