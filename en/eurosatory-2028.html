Preparation dossier · Defence & security

Maximizing survival in dead zones.

A mobile, instrumented and scalable approach to protecting personnel exposed to aerial threats and dangerous terrain.

Discover the system

A mobile sentinel

The system combines a quadruped mobile robot, very-low-latency multi-frequency gyroscopic detection and a precision response module.

Positioned ahead of or around a team, it creates a first layer between the threat and human personnel while maintaining an alert capability in unstable or exposed areas.

Performance, sensor integration, rules of engagement and regulatory compliance must be defined and validated with industrial partners and competent authorities.

Configurations

A / Compact · 100 m³

For an isolated post, a critical access point, an airlock or a constrained passage.

B / Recommended balance · 500 m³

A compromise between mobility, coverage and response capacity for a moving team.

C / Extended · 1,000 m³

For a larger footprint, logistics area, support point or perimeter.

Ground-detection option

An additional module could extend the robot’s mission to ground reconnaissance and the detection of indicators of antipersonnel mines.

Next step
