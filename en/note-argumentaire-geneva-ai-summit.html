Road to Geneva — discussion paper

Shared Prosperity and Trust in AI

A discussion paper for the workshop.

EventGeneva AI Summit 2027
LanguagesFrench and English

Shared prosperity and trust in AI are not two separate objectives. Trust conditions the adoption of AI that benefits the greatest number, while shared prosperity gives that trust concrete credibility.

The starting point

An innovation that benefits only a few actors cannot inspire lasting trust. Conversely, trust proclaimed without visible effects on people’s lives, work and access to opportunities remains fragile.

Trust based on guarantees

American, Chinese, European and Swiss approaches rely on different understandings of freedom, responsibility, authority, safety and the common good. The workshop will therefore examine guarantees that can be verified: clear responsibility, supervision and appeal, risk identification and genuine participation.

Plurality as a method

Differences between these frameworks are not obstacles to hide. They are starting points for asking where disagreements lie, which concerns are shared and which guarantees can be formulated without erasing differences in values and governance.

A philosophical perspective

Descartes, Pascal, Leibniz and Spinoza offer useful questions about knowledge, decision-making, freedom, responsibility, authority and collective power to act. The philosophical perspective remains in service of the practical debate.

A structured discussion

  1. Framing: recall the Summit’s two priorities and their interdependence.
  2. Structured exchange: compare plural viewpoints around American, Chinese, European and Swiss approaches.
  3. Synthesis: identify common reference points for the Geneva AI Summit.

Expected outcome

The workshop is not intended to produce a general consensus statement. It should produce a concise set of principles, guarantees and open questions capable of connecting shared prosperity, safety and trust.
