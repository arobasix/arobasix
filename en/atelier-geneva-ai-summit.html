Geneva AI Summit 2027

Shared Prosperity and Trust in AI

An online workshop bringing the two priorities of the Geneva AI Summit into the same discussion, rather than treating them as separate themes.

DateWednesday, 10 February 2027
Time14:00–15:00 CET
FormatOnline meeting via Zoom
LanguageFrench / English

Register

The Summit’s two priorities are not separate themes: trust conditions the adoption of shared prosperity, while shared prosperity gives trust its credibility.

Why this workshop?

What counts as responsible and safe AI differs depending on the region from which it is considered. American, Chinese, European and Swiss frameworks offer different readings, sometimes difficult to reconcile.

The workshop brings deliberately varied viewpoints into direct discussion, with the aim of identifying common ground around prosperity for all and trust in the use of AI.

Programme

14:00 — Framing
Presentation of the Summit’s two priorities and the point of tension between them.
14:10 — Structured exchange
Discussion of American, Chinese, European and Swiss approaches.
14:45 — Synthesis
Identify common reference points and formulate them as a contribution to the Summit.
